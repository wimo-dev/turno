What a person sees in the browser comes in Spanish when the identity's locale is Spanish (`es`, `es-MX`, `es-419`; else the first of `languages`): the live view page and its messages, the `browser_request_human` banner and its Done button, the agent cursor's badge and the welcome page of `turno-browser login`. Any other locale, or none, keeps English. Tool results, tool descriptions and CLI output stay in English. The live view now shows a person step's pause as "Paused (your turn)" instead of the tool name.
